Types of Digital Payments

Five common ways to pay without cash.

📲

UPI Payments

Send money instantly using a UPI app with a mobile number, UPI ID, or QR code.

💳

Debit and Credit Cards

Pay at shops or online using the card details and a secure code.

🏦

Internet Banking

Manage your bank account on a computer, with transfers and bill payments.

👛

Mobile Wallets

Store money in a wallet app and use it to pay bills, recharges, and shops.

📱

Mobile Banking

Use your bank's official app to check balance, transfer money, and pay bills.

Common Online Frauds

Know the tricks so you can recognise them quickly.

🧾

Fake UPI Payment Requests

Receiving money never needs your UPI PIN. If someone asks for it, it is a scam.

✉️

Phishing Messages

Fake SMS or emails with links that copy a bank website to steal your login details.

🔢

OTP Scams

A caller pretends to be from your bank and asks for the OTP sent to your phone.

📷

QR Code Scams

A QR code sent "to receive money" actually takes money out of your account.

☎️

Fake Customer Care Calls

Fake helpline numbers appear in online search results. Use the number printed on your bank's official site.

🖥️

Remote Access Scams

Someone asks you to install an app that lets them control your phone to "fix" a problem.

Digital Payment Safety Tips

Simple habits that protect your money every day.

  • 🔐
    Never share your OTP or UPI PIN. Not with bank staff, friends, or anyone else.
  • 🔍
    Verify the receiver before payment. Check the name shown before you confirm.
  • 🚫
    Never click suspicious links. Delete messages that promise prizes or urgent action.
  • ✅
    Use official payment applications. Download them only from the official app store.
  • 🔔
    Check transaction notifications. Read every SMS and alert so you spot unknown payments.
  • 📵
    Avoid unknown QR codes. Scan only codes shown by a shop or person you are paying.

What To Do If Fraud Happens

Act fast. Quick action can help you recover money.

1

Contact your bank immediately

Call the official number on your card or bank website and ask them to block the payment.

2

Call 1930

This is the national cybercrime helpline for reporting online financial fraud in India.

3

Report online

File a complaint on the official portal at cybercrime.gov.in.

4

Save evidence

Keep transaction IDs, screenshots, messages, call logs, and the time of the incident.
